## Setting up
1. Position an adjustable bench at a low incline angle (e.g., near 30 degrees) and set a pair of dumbbells under the head of the bench.
    1. As a different variation, which you can use if your bench does not have a low incline setting, you can alternatively select a moderate incline angle (e.g., near 45 degrees) and otherwise use the same technique for this exercise.
2. Lie down with your chest contacting the head of the bench and your legs spread out as you position the balls of your feet on the ground.
3. Grasp the dumbbells, position your arms so that they are perpendicular to the ground with extended elbows, and rotate your arms so that your palms are facing forward.
    1. Keep your elbows extended and your palms facing this direction throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Raise your arms upward and straight out to your sides through your available shoulder range of motion so that they form a “T-shape” with your torso as you continue to keep your chest in contact with the bench.
    1. You can strive to raise your arms until they are at least parallel to the floor, but your shoulder mobility will affect how high you can raise your arms.
    2. If you notice your chest lifting up off the bench during this movement, that indicates that you most likely are dynamically extending your lumbar spine, which can occur as a compensation pattern instead of lifting your arm only with your shoulder girdle muscles.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering your arms in a controlled manner until your arms have returned to being perpendicular to the ground.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise trains your [rear delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Posterior%20Head%201905c6f170d880a29e5ec26f4d257c01.md), [traps](../Muscles/Trapezius%201b95c6f170d880bebf89c759b592e81f.md), and [rhomboids](../Muscles/Rhomboids%201b95c6f170d880c8a84de94f5232a509.md), which are assisted by your rotator cuff’s [infraspinatus](../Muscles/Infraspinatus%201905c6f170d88026aebcf3953e2e440f.md), [teres minor](../Muscles/Teres%20Minor%201905c6f170d88098824dc8136557c410.md), and [supraspinatus](../Muscles/Supraspinatus%201905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97.md).
