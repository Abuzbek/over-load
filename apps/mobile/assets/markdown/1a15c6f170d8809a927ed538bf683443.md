## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of kettlebells and stand upright with approximately a shoulder width stance while holding the kettlebells at your sides with extended elbows.
2. Direct your palms so that they are facing forward.
    1. Maintain this underhand grip on the kettlebells throughout the set.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl both kettlebells upward by bending at your elbows while keeping your upper arms fixed at your sides (rather than allowing your upper arms to swing forward).
2. After your elbows have fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the kettlebells in a controlled manner until your elbows have returned to an extended position.
    1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arms positioned by your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the kettlebells up and lowering them down until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your [biceps](../Muscles/Biceps%20Brachii%201b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68.md), [brachialis](../Muscles/Brachialis%2018f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9.md), and [brachioradialis](../Muscles/Brachioradialis%201905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3.md).
