## Setting up
1. Slide a pair of fat grip attachments onto two dumbbells, and firmly grasp them.
2. In an approximately hip width stance, stand upright and hold the dumbbells by your sides with straight elbows as your palms are facing in toward your sides (resulting in a neutral grip that is also called a hammer grip).
## Exercise technique
1. Curl the dumbbells upward in an arcing manner by bending your elbows while keeping palms facing in toward each other.
    1. Keep your upper arms fixed by your sides rather than allowing them to swing forward through shoulder motion.
    2. While your forearms move through elbow joint motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
    3. Maintain a tight grip on the dumbbells with straight wrists throughout the entirety of this exercise.
    4. At the top of the curl, your elbows should be fully bent with your forearms touching your biceps.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner until your have straightened your elbows.
    1. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the dumbbells down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
While dynamically training your [biceps](../Muscles/Biceps%20Brachii%201b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68.md), [brachialis](../Muscles/Brachialis%2018f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9.md), and [brachioradialis](../Muscles/Brachioradialis%201905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3.md), this exercise can statically train your [forearm’s extrinsic finger flexors](../Muscles/Extrinsic%20Finger%20Flexors%201bb5c6f170d88050a550fbeff6041573.md) and your [hand’s intrinsic finger flexors](../Muscles/Intrinsic%20Finger%20Flexors%201bb5c6f170d880648d61d9b5db235bf6.md).
