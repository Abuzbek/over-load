## Setting up
1. Adjust the height of the seat so that the handles are level with your shoulders once you are seated with an upright torso.
2. Adjust the lever arms to position the handles as close together (on the side of the chest pad) as your machine enables.
3. Select your desired weight with the loading pin.
4. Sit down sideways on the seat with what will be your working side further away from the chest pad.
    1. Rest your feet on the floor with your knees bent.
5. With your arm further away from the chest pad, reach across your body to grasp the horizontal handle with your palm facing down. Simultaneously turn your chest toward the handle to help you reach it.
    1. To grasp the handle, you can initially stand up before sitting back down during the next step if that helps you reach it.
6. While holding the handle with a straight or slightly bent elbow, turn your chest until it is facing the same direction as your imaginary belt buckle.
    1. In this starting position, your working arm should be situated fully across your body, which should prompt you to feel a stretching sensation in the back of your shoulder.
    2. Position your free hand wherever you prefer throughout the exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. Without bending your elbow, smoothly sweep your arm outward and across your body in a wide arcing motion.
    1. For the standard version of this exercise, you can finish once your arm is directed straight out to the side of your shoulder, resulting in your torso and arm forming half of a “T-shape.”
    2. While you move your arm through shoulder motion, keep the rest of your body stationary throughout your set.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your arm to sweep fluidly in toward the starting position through the full range of motion your shoulder mobility permits.
    1. Just like when you finished setting up, you should feel a stretching sensation in the back of your shoulder as your arm is fully across your body.
    2. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should actively resist the weight rather than letting it abruptly yank your arm inward.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your [rear delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Posterior%20Head%201905c6f170d880a29e5ec26f4d257c01.md), which is assisted by your [traps](../Muscles/Trapezius%201b95c6f170d880bebf89c759b592e81f.md) and [rhomboids](../Muscles/Rhomboids%201b95c6f170d880c8a84de94f5232a509.md), along with your rotator cuff’s [infraspinatus](../Muscles/Infraspinatus%201905c6f170d88026aebcf3953e2e440f.md), [teres minor](../Muscles/Teres%20Minor%201905c6f170d88098824dc8136557c410.md), and [supraspinatus](../Muscles/Supraspinatus%201905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97.md).
