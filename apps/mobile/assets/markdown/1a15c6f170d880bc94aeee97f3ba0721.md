## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and stand upright with approximately a shoulder width stance while holding the dumbbells at your sides with extended elbows.
2. Direct your palms so that they are facing in toward your sides (i.e., using a neutral grip position).
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping one dumbbell resting at your side with an extended elbow, curl the other dumbbell upward by bending at your elbow while keeping your upper arm fixed at your side (rather than allowing your upper arm to swing forward).
    1. While curling the dumbbell upward, simultaneously rotate your forearm into an underhand grip position that results in your palm facing back toward you when your elbow is fully flexed.
2. After your elbow has fully flexed and your forearm has rotated from a neutral grip to an underhand grip, reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbell in a controlled manner until your elbow has returned to an extended position.
    1. While lowering the dumbbell, simultaneously rotate your forearm back into a neutral grip position that results in your palm facing in toward your side when your elbow is fully extended.
    2. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arm positioned by your side.
3. With your other arm, repeat steps 3 and 4.
4. Alternate between curling with each side until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your [biceps](../Muscles/Biceps%20Brachii%201b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68.md), [brachialis](../Muscles/Brachialis%2018f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9.md), and [brachioradialis](../Muscles/Brachioradialis%201905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3.md).
