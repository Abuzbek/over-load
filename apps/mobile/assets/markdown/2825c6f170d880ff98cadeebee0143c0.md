Kipping proficiently involves transitioning efficiently from an arched body position to a hollow body position. Learning how to perform kipping pull-ups can be a meaningfully easier task if you have initially developed competency in achieving these two positions independently, which training the [hollow body hold](../Exercises/Hollow%20body%20hold%202815c6f170d8802483f6dec09280d25f.md) and the [superman](../Exercises/Superman%201a45c6f170d8807cae53dd7f488cd0f3.md) exercise can provide. If you have a difficult time performing either exercise by itself in a controlled manner, it can be rather challenging to integrate them together into a coordinated, higher velocity movement.
