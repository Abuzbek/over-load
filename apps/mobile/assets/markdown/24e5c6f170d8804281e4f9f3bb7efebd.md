If you have limited shoulder mobility, it may be prudent to address your mobility limitations prior to performing challenging behind the neck press sets. This exercise is not inherently injurious to the shoulders, but it requires more shoulder mobility to perform effectively than most pressing variations. Exercises like the [scapular wall slide](../Exercises/Scapular%20wall%20slide%201a15c6f170d8806490d0e768051691f1.md) may be of assistance in helping to improve the shoulder mobility that is required to perform behind the neck press comfortably.
