## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and stand upright with approximately a shoulder-width stance while holding the dumbbells at your sides with slightly bent elbows as your palms are facing in toward your sides (i.e., in a neutral grip position).
2. Slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips.
    1. Maintain this position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Raise the dumbbells out and away from your sides until your upper arms are close to parallel with the ground.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner back to your sides.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your [lateral delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Lateral%20Head%201905c6f170d8804b9b6ef12253eec774.md) and [supraspinatus](../Muscles/Supraspinatus%201905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97.md) while also training your [front delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Anterior%20Head%201905c6f170d8805da59fc8f9dc0a59e4.md) and [upper trap](../Muscles/Upper%20Trapezius%201925c6f170d880009e2cf04b5b29b467.md).
