While not as challenging to learn as Olympic lift variations such as the snatch and split jerk, the barbell push press is not easy to develop technical proficiency in. Prior to attempting the barbell push press, it can be quite beneficial to accrue some experience with the [clean grip front squat](../Exercises/Full%20hand%20clean%20grip%20front%20squat%201a25c6f170d880068daae293704193e0.md) and the strict version of the [standing barbell overhead press](../Exercises/Barbell%20overhead%20press%201a05c6f170d880c5ad11c99b302b1bf9.md). Once you are reasonably competent in performing those two exercises, you can more readily learn how to execute the barbell push press.

If you are unable to achieve a stable front rack position with a clean grip, you likely have mobility restrictions in your wrists and/or shoulders that will limit the degree to which your legs can assist in driving the bar up. In addition to statically stretching your wrists and/or shoulders, repeatedly working into as much of the front rack position as you can without pain can improve your mobility over time. It is common for people to lack the mobility to assume a stable front rack position with a clean grip when attempting it for the first time, so there is no need to become discouraged if you cannot initially do so. If your mobility currently limits your ability to perform the barbell push press, you also have the option of training the [dumbbell push press](../Exercises/Dumbbell%20push%20press%201a05c6f170d880e58be2eda50377286f.md) in the meantime.
