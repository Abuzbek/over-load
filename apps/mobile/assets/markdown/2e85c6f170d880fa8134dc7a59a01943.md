## Setting up
1. Position an adjustable exercise bench to an incline of about 30-45 degrees.
    1. A 30-degree incline can provide greater resistance to your delts in the starting position of each rep than a 45-degree incline, but the moderately higher incline is still completely viable for this exercise.
    2. As an alternative to an adjustable bench, you have the option of using a fixed moderate incline bench.
2. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on the incline bench with your feet flat on the ground.
    1. Keep your upper back in contact with the bench throughout the set. Based on your preference, you can rest your head on the bench or keep it slightly above it.
    2. While you do not necessarily need to have an exaggerated “proud chest” posture with your shoulders fully pulled back, avoid sitting with your shoulders rolled forward into a slouched upper back position.
3. With your palms angled in toward each other and slightly backward, direct your arms down toward the ground with straight or slightly bent elbows.
    1. Whether you keep your elbows straight or slightly bent is up to your preference, but you should statically maintain your preferred elbow position throughout the set.
    2. You can perform this step simultaneously with step 2.
## Exercise technique
1. In a smooth arcing motion, sweep your arms diagonally upward, outward, and mildly forward through a range of motion comfortable to your shoulders.
    1. Lifting until the hands are near ear height is often appropriate, but you can determine specifically how high up you finish based on your personal preference and what feels comfortable. The right range of motion for you should not prompt you to feel any shoulder discomfort in the top position.
    2. As you raise your arms through shoulder motion and allow your shoulder blades to move naturally, the rest of your body should remain stationary.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering the dumbbells in toward your sides in a controlled manner.
    1. You should finish in the starting position with your arms pointing down toward the ground.
    2. While you do not need to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the weights downward.
    3. The dumbbells should predominantly arc downward and inward, while simultaneously tracking slightly backward to reverse the forward motion that occurred during step 1.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise primarily trains your [lateral delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Lateral%20Head%201905c6f170d8804b9b6ef12253eec774.md), [front delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Anterior%20Head%201905c6f170d8805da59fc8f9dc0a59e4.md), and [supraspinatus](../Muscles/Supraspinatus%201905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97.md), which are assisted by your [upper trap](../Muscles/Upper%20Trapezius%201925c6f170d880009e2cf04b5b29b467.md).
