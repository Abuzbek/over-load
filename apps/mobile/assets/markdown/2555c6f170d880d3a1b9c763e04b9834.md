With a certain amount of weight, getting into and out of position for a decline dumbbell press without a spotter may be more challenging than performing the exercise itself. Performing higher rep sets with a lighter load can reduce this challenge to a degree, but you can alternatively select a different decline variation, such as the [decline single arm dumbbell bench press](../Exercises/Decline%20single%20arm%20dumbbell%20press%201a05c6f170d88061a44edbcda249b9d4.md), which will naturally be easier to get into and out of position with the lighter load. The [slight decline dumbbell bench press](../Exercises/Slight%20decline%20dumbbell%20bench%20press%202535c6f170d8801bab2af2748abfe4e1.md) can additionally allow you to train decline pressing with a meaningfully lower difficulty for getting into and out of position.
