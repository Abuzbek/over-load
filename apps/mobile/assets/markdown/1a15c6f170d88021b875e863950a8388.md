## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a bench with an upright posture and your feet flat on the floor while holding the dumbbells at your sides with extended elbows as your palms are facing in toward your sides (i.e., in a neutral grip position).
    1. You can opt to sit down on a flat bench without any back support or sit down on a seated bench with back support.
## Exercise technique
1. Raise the dumbbells out in front of you until your upper arms are approximately parallel with the ground.
    1. If you wish to do so, elevating the dumbbells higher up, beyond when your upper arms are parallel to the floor, is a viable modification to this exercise’s technique.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner back to your sides.
3. Alternate between raising the dumbbells up and lowering them down until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your [front delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Anterior%20Head%201905c6f170d8805da59fc8f9dc0a59e4.md) while also training your [upper pec major](../Muscles/Pectoralis%20Major%20(Clavicular%20Head%201905c6f170d880ef9b6bd48adbf440bd.md), [lateral delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Lateral%20Head%201905c6f170d8804b9b6ef12253eec774.md), and [supraspinatus](../Muscles/Supraspinatus%201905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97.md).
