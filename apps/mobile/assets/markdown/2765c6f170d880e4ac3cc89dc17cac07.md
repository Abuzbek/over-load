## Setting up
1. Sit on the ground with your knees bent by approximately 90 degrees and your soles flat on the ground about hip-width apart.
2. Place your hands on the ground approximately shoulder-width apart, slightly behind your hips, with your fingers spread apart and elbows straight.
    1. Rotating your hands such that your middle fingers point straight out to your sides is appropriate. However, you can feel free to rotate your hands to point in whichever direction you find most comfortable.
    2. The proper set-up should allow your arms and shins to point straight down toward the ground during the exercise.
3. Pull your shoulder blades back together to assume a “proud chest” posture.
    1. Rather than being slumped forward, your chest should be directed slightly upward.
    2. As a cue, you can think about pinching a pencil with your shoulder blades.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heels into the ground and squeezing your glutes until your torso and thighs are parallel to the ground.
    1. At the top, you should be able to draw a straight line from your knees through your hips to your shoulders when viewed from the side.
    2. Keep your elbows straight as the weight you bear through your arms predominantly goes through the bases of your palms (AKA heels of your hands) rather than closer to your knuckles.
    3. Continue keeping your shoulder blades pulled back with a “proud chest” as you avoid letting your shoulders round forward.
    4. Keep your neck near a neutral position, like when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
    5. Squeezing your glutes can help you avoid excessively arching your lower back, which should exhibit its natural mild curvature. As a cue for engaging your glutes, you can think about trying to crush a walnut between them.
2. Statically hold the rigid position you adopted from step 1 until you have completed your set’s target duration.
    1. Strive to have your body look the same during the last second of your set as it did during the first second after getting into position.
    2. Resist letting your hips gradually lower toward the ground as time elapses.
3. After completing your set, bend your hips to return to sitting on the ground.
## Description
This exercise can statically train your [gluteus maximus](../Muscles/Gluteus%20Maximus%201bb5c6f170d8805380e7c611cd051f9f.md) and [spinal extensor muscles](../Muscles/Thoracolumbar%20Erector%20Spinae%201bb5c6f170d880a7a719e8f6d05d00a8.md) that run along your entire back.
