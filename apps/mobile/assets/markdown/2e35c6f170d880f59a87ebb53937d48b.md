Specifically with behind-the-neck lat pulldowns, people often find that a wider grip feels more comfortable on their shoulders than a moderate grip. Regardless of grip width, some people lack the mobility required to perform behind-the-neck pulldowns without shoulder or other joint discomfort. If you find that to be the case for you, you can readily perform a different lat pulldown variation, such as the standard [overhand wide grip lat pulldown](../Exercises/Wide%20grip%20cable%20lat%20pulldown%201a15c6f170d8809d8cdbf9394bfb079c.md) that is performed to the chest rather than behind the neck.
