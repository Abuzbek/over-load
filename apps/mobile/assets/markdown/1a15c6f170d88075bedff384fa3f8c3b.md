## Setting up
1. Using an overhand grip, grasp an elastic band with both hands in front of your torso while standing upright with your feet in approximately a shoulder-width stance.
    1. Inspect the band to confirm it does not have any small tears, since a small tear can swiftly become a full tear when stretching a band.
2. Position your hands so that they are somewhat greater than shoulder width apart from each other, with slack taken out of the band.
3. With straight elbows, elevate your arms in front of your torso until they are approximately parallel to the ground.
    1. In this position, your palms should be directed down toward the ground.
    2. Maintain this position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Pull your hands apart and backward until your arms and torso form a “T-shape.”
    1. If you set up by grasping the band with your hands too close together, you may reach the limit of the band’s extensibility (as indicated by an abrupt increase in resistance from the band) prior to your hands being pulled entirely apart from each other. If you notice this occurring, bring your hands back together and adjust your setup by gripping the band with your hands further apart. Continuing to pull the band apart once a portion has fully stretched may cause the band to tear.
2. Reverse the motion by bringing your hands forward and toward each other until they have returned to their starting position.
    1. You should feel tension from the band throughout the entire range of motion.
    2. Do not bring your hands so close together that the band becomes slack.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise trains your [rear delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Posterior%20Head%201905c6f170d880a29e5ec26f4d257c01.md), which is assisted by your [traps](../Muscles/Trapezius%201b95c6f170d880bebf89c759b592e81f.md) and [rhomboids](../Muscles/Rhomboids%201b95c6f170d880c8a84de94f5232a509.md), along with your rotator cuff’s [infraspinatus](../Muscles/Infraspinatus%201905c6f170d88026aebcf3953e2e440f.md), [teres minor](../Muscles/Teres%20Minor%201905c6f170d88098824dc8136557c410.md), and [supraspinatus](../Muscles/Supraspinatus%201905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97.md).
