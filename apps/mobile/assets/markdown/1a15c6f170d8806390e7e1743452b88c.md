## Setting up
1. Prepare the barbell with your desired load by adding weight plates to it or leaving the barbell unloaded, which is recommended if performing this exercise for the first time.
    1. You can initially set the bar up on the floor or from an elevated position, such as from a power rack.
2. While facing the middle of the bar, squat down or hinge at your hips with extended elbows.
3. Grasp the bar with approximately a shoulder width grip and an overhand grip such that your palms are directed toward you.
4. While keeping your elbows extended, stand upright while gripping the bar.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl the bar upward by bending at your elbows while keeping your upper arms fixed at your sides (rather than allowing your upper arms to swing forward).
2. After your elbows have fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until your elbows have returned to an extended position.
    1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arms positioned by your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the bar up and lowering it down until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can develop your [biceps](../Muscles/Biceps%20Brachii%201b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68.md), [brachialis](../Muscles/Brachialis%2018f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9.md), and [brachioradialis](../Muscles/Brachioradialis%201905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3.md), but it may train your biceps somewhat less effectively than a curl with an underhand or neutral grip.
