The diamond push-up is a more advanced variation than the standard push-up, and it has been measured to produce greater average [EMG activity](../Sources/COMPARISON%20OF%20MUSCLE%20ACTIVATION%20USING%20VARIOUS%20HAND%202335c6f170d88060b8f9c48de8d4fd52.md) in both the triceps and pec major than the standard push-up or wide grip push-up. Given the greater difficulty per rep, it is strongly recommended that you become proficient with standard push-ups, performed through a full range of motion with quality technique, before attempting diamond push-ups.
