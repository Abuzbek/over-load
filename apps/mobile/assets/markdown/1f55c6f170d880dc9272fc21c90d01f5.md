## Setting up
1. Position yourself with your palms, knees, and toes on the ground as your elbows are straight, and your knees are bent.
    1. Your upper arms should be directed straight down such that they are approximately perpendicular to the ground.
## Exercise technique
1. Straighten one knee to move your foot behind you and position the bottom of its toes onto the ground before doing the same with your other leg.
    1. You should now be in the same position as the top of a push-up.
2. Tighten your abdominal muscles and squeeze your glutes to maintain a flat back position while keeping your elbows and knees straight.
    1. Doing so keeps your hips held up (but not excessively hiked up) rather than letting them sag downward, which would result in an exaggerated arch of your lower back.
    2. In this position, you should be able to draw a straight line from your legs through hips to your torso.
    3. Do not allow your chest to sag, which would result in your shoulder blades being pinched back together.
    4. Keep your neck in a neutral position, as you would normally do when standing upright.
3. While keeping your knees and elbows straightened, lift one leg and the opposite arm slightly up off the ground and hold that position briefly as you keep your abdominal muscles and glutes tightened to maintain the same rigid trunk position you adopted in step 2.
    1. While you move your leg at your hip and your opposite arm at your shoulder, the rest of your body should be as close to stationary as is physically possible for you.
    2. When maintaining the appropriately rigid trunk position, someone should be able to place an object, such as a mug of water, on top of your back without the object falling off your back as you lift your leg.
    3. Particularly avoid letting your lower back dynamically arch or letting your hip bone (on the side of the leg you are lifting) tilt up.
    4. Do not lift your leg and arm any higher than the range that allows you to keep your trunk in a rigid, static position.
    5. You can aim to hold your leg and arm off the ground for at least a couple of seconds, but it is up to you specifically how long you wish to maintain that position.
4. Return your leg and arm to the ground in a controlled manner before repeating step 3 with your other leg and arm.
5. Alternate between steps 3 and 4 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise statically trains your core muscles to maintain a rigid trunk position as you refine your ability to move through your hips and shoulders with a stable trunk.
