## Setting up
1. At a dual cable station, adjust each pulley to its lowest height, connect a D-handle attachment, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. After standing centered between the cables with your sides facing the cable stacks, side step to grasp the handle on one side with your opposite hand, grasp the other handle with your free hand, and return to being centered between the cables.
    1. Your palms should be approximately facing each other as your hands are situated just in front of your thighs.
3. Slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips and slightly bend your elbows.
    1. Maintain this position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Raise the handles out and away from your sides until your upper arms are slightly below parallel with the ground.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the handles back to your sides in a controlled manner.
3. Alternate between rsteps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your [lateral delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Lateral%20Head%201905c6f170d8804b9b6ef12253eec774.md) and [supraspinatus](../Muscles/Supraspinatus%201905c6f170d880efb440c1614caf1c97.md), which are assisted by your [front delt](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Anterior%20Head%201905c6f170d8805da59fc8f9dc0a59e4.md) and [upper trap](../Muscles/Upper%20Trapezius%201925c6f170d880009e2cf04b5b29b467.md).
