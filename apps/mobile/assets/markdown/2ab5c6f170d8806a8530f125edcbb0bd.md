## Setting up
1. Position an adjustable exercise bench at a moderate incline near 45 degrees, and pick up a pair of dumbbells.
    1. Alternatively, you can use a fixed moderate incline bench for this exercise.
    2. Any bench angle that is greater than 30 degrees but less than 60 degrees can be considered a moderate incline.
2. Lie down with your chest contacting the head of the bench and your legs spread out as you position the balls of your feet on the ground.
    1. With the appropriate positioning, your feet should feel stable on the ground, and your torso should be well-supported by the bench.
    2. Shorter lifters may be able to assume this position with straight knees, but taller lifters may have to bend their knees in order to rest their chest comfortably on the head of the bench.
3. Allow your arms to hang down toward the ground with straight elbows as your palms are angled diagonally in toward each other and backward.
    1. Maintain this grip (which is a hybrid between an overhand grip and a neutral grip) throughout the exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. Sweep your arms diagonally backward and outward away from each other in an arcing motion through the full range your shoulder mobility permits.
    1. Continue keeping your elbows straightened and chest in contact with the bench throughout the exercise.
    2. Strive to sweep your arms equal parts backward and outward, which should result in your arms forming approximately 45-degree angles near the top of the exercise when viewed from behind.
    3. When moving through your full range of motion, your arms should finish at least slightly behind/above your torso.
    4. While you lift your arms through shoulder motion and allow your shoulder blades to move naturally, the rest of your body should remain stationary.
2. Reverse the movement in a controlled manner by lowering your arms diagonally forward and inward until they are pointing straight down toward the ground.
    1. You should finish with dumbbells vertically aligned under your shoulders.
    2. While you do not need to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the weights downward.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your [rear delts](../Muscles/Deltoid%20Posterior%20Head%201905c6f170d880a29e5ec26f4d257c01.md) while also training your [traps](../Muscles/Trapezius%201b95c6f170d880bebf89c759b592e81f.md), [rhomboids](../Muscles/Rhomboids%201b95c6f170d880c8a84de94f5232a509.md), [lats](../Muscles/Latissimus%20Dorsi%201905c6f170d880109e73f99ab91d4d47.md), [teres majores](../Muscles/Teres%20Major%201905c6f170d880dbba32f6bd22e3abe6.md), and two [rotator cuff](../Muscles/Rotator%20Cuff%201b95c6f170d880b3b0d8eab69c3a7683.md) muscles to a lesser degree.
