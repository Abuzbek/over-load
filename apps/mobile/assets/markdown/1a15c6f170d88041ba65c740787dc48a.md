## Setting up
1. Pick up a dumbbell and stand upright with approximately a shoulder width stance while holding the dumbbell at your side with an extended elbow.
2. Direct your palm so that it is facing forward.
    1. Maintain this underhand grip on the dumbbell throughout the set.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl the dumbbell upward by bending at your elbow while keeping your upper arm fixed at your side (rather than allowing your upper arm to swing forward).
2. After your elbow has fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbell in a controlled manner until your elbow has returned to an extended position.
    1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arm positioned by your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the dumbbell up and lowering it down until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your [biceps](../Muscles/Biceps%20Brachii%201b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68.md), [brachialis](../Muscles/Brachialis%2018f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9.md), and [brachioradialis](../Muscles/Brachioradialis%201905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3.md).
