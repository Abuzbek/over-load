## Setting up
1. Situate a pair of dumbbells on an elevated surface (like an exercise bench) or the ground with their handles oriented vertically from one side of each dumbbell facing up.
2. Spread your fingers apart, and firmly grasp the top end of each dumbbell with your palms directed down.
3. In a near hip-width stance, stand upright with your arms by your sides and your elbows straight.
## Exercise technique
1. While forcefully squeezing the dumbbells, curl them upward in an arcing manner by bending your elbows. Strive to keep the dumbbells’ handles fairly aligned with your forearms, such that the handles and your forearms point in a similar direction throughout the curl.
    1. When your elbows are bent by about 90 degrees, and your forearms are in a horizontal position, the dumbbells’ handles should be mostly horizontal. This will require cocking your wrists back and pointing your palms forward rather than down toward the ground with straight wrists, which would situate the handles in a vertical position.
    2. When your elbows are fully bent at the top of the curl, the dumbbells’ handles should be angled upward like your forearms.
    3. Keep your upper arms fixed by your sides rather than allowing them to swing forward through shoulder motion.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner until you have straightened your elbows. Continue keeping the handles pointing in a similar direction as your forearms while you resist letting your wrists straighten.
    1. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the dumbbells down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
While dynamically training your [biceps](../Muscles/Biceps%20Brachii%201b95c6f170d88027b895ed4c0bb40b68.md), [brachialis](../Muscles/Brachialis%2018f5c6f170d880c181eed18b5b379fe9.md), and [brachioradialis](../Muscles/Brachioradialis%201905c6f170d8808f8207e301c4acd3b3.md), this exercise can statically train your hand's [intrinsic finger flexors](../Muscles/Intrinsic%20Finger%20Flexors%201bb5c6f170d880648d61d9b5db235bf6.md) and your forearm’s [wrist extensors](../Muscles/Wrist%20extensors%202c35c6f170d8804ab377fe1e53371f25.md).
