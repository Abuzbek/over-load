While a full range of motion seated calf raises can serve as a useful means of improving ankle mobility that may transfer to squatting, they are noticeably less effective than straight knee calf raises (like standing calf raises) for inducing total calf muscle growth. There is [reason](../Sources/Muscle%20swelling%20of%20the%20triceps%20surae%20in%20response%20t%2022c5c6f170d8808eb6f0f585fce4b6ed.md) to believe that seated calf raises may train the soleus (which only crosses the ankle joint and exclusively functions as an ankle plantarflexor) to a slightly greater degree than straight knee calf raises. However, two studies ([one](../Sources/Triceps%20surae%20muscle%20hypertrophy%20is%20greater%20after%20%201ac5c6f170d880d2aef7d505cc3b397f.md), [two](../Sources/Exercise%20Selection%20Differentially%20Influences%20Lower%201fc5c6f170d8802bb469e3e22c476585.md)) have found that seated and straight knee calf raises elicit statistically similar magnitudes of soleus hypertrophy. On the other hand, the gastrocnemii (which cross both the ankle and knee joints and function both as ankle plantarflexors and knee flexors) have been reported to grow considerably more from straight knee calf raises compared to seated calf raises.

Because they function as knee flexors, a straight knee position allows the gastrocnemii to be loaded at longer lengths, which is conducive to muscle development. While the soleus is the largest calf muscle, the two gastrocnemii have been [measured](../Sources/Relationships%20of%2035%20lower%20limb%20muscles%20to%20height%20a%201bd5c6f170d88007b374cd8a7cff2cd8.md) to have a combined muscle volume that is only slightly lower than the volume of the soleus. Furthermore, the soleus appears to have a lower capacity for muscle growth than the gastrocnemii, which is likely influenced by the soleus having a very high proportion of [slow twitch muscle fibers](../Sources/Human%20soleus%20muscle%20A%20comparison%20of%20fiber%20composit%2022c5c6f170d880bca31cd4a137067591.md) in comparison to the gastrocnemii having a more even balance between slow and fast twitch fibers. Consequently, straight knee calf raises can be meaningfully more effective for increasing total calf muscle size relative to seated calf raises.
